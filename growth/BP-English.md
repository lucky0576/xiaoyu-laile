## 1. The Problem

China's small and mid-sized industrial companies (EPC contractors, equipment makers,
traders, developers) still source and sell projects through WeChat groups and personal
relationships. The information is:

- **Scattered** — thousands of WeChat groups, no search, no archive.
- **Unverifiable** — no way to tell a real project from a forwarded rumour.
- **Wasted** — a project owner with a 200 MW solar project cannot find qualified
  EPC contractors outside their own network, and vice versa.

Result: deal origination costs are high, and small companies are locked out of
large projects entirely.

---

## 2. The Solution

**Xiaoyu Laile is a vertical information and matching platform for Chinese
industrial projects** — and, from 2026, a short-video-first product.

| Layer | What we do |
|---|---|
| **Information** | Projects, tenders, trade, academic results and events, structured with AI (title, summary, industry, city, budget, qualification requirements). |
| **Matching** | AI search across the full database ("find me storage projects in Zhejiang above 100 MWh"), plus one-click broadcast packs for WeChat groups. |
| **Short video** | **Every listing can be turned into a 12–15 second vertical video automatically** — with the project title burned in and a QR code to the listing. No video production skills needed. |
| **Monetisation** | Enterprise services (due diligence reports, regional data, private deployment), city/industry/channel **stewards** (annual licence), and membership. |

## 4. Market

- China has **>50 million** SMEs in construction, manufacturing and trading.
- Project-based industries (EPC, new energy, environmental, municipal) rely on
  relationship-driven sourcing; there is no dominant vertical information platform.
- Short video is now the default information channel for Chinese SME owners:
  Douyin/WeChat Channels have become where business owners discover suppliers.
- **Our wedge**: nobody produces short videos for *project opportunities*. We can,
  automatically, for every listing — because our data is already structured.

**Adjacent validation**: tender aggregation (千里马, 剑鱼标讯), B2B directories
(慧聪, 阿里巴巴), and short-video agencies all exist, but none combine
*structured project data + automated short video + regional stewards*.

## 6. Why Now

1. **AI made content production free.** We generate titles, summaries, and now
   videos, at near-zero marginal cost. A 3-person competitor cannot keep up.
2. **Short video is where SME owners are.** We are moving from a directory to a
   video feed, which changes unit economics (ad inventory + organic reach).
3. **Compliance window.** China now requires micro-drama filing and, for
   monetisation, ICP filing. We are building the compliance stack (entity,
   copyright, filing) before scaling — most informal competitors will be squeezed out.

## 7. Competition

| Player | What they do | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Tender aggregators (千里马/剑鱼) | Collect public tenders | No user-generated projects, no video, no matching |
| 慧聪 / 阿里巴巴 B2B | Product listings | Not project-based, no local stewards |
| WeChat groups | Everything, manually | No structure, no search, no verification |
| Short-video agencies | Produce brand videos | Not scalable, don't touch project data |

**Moat (early, but real):** structured project data + AI generation pipeline +
regional steward network. Each city steward adds local supply that competitors
cannot copy without a physical presence.
